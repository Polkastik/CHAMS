FAQs

HOW CAN WE HELP?
How do I submit a new support ticket?
To submit a new support ticket, navigate to the "Home" or "Tickets" dashboard and locate the Create Ticket button. Fill out the required details including the subject, category, and a detailed description of the issue. Click Submit once complete.
Where can I view the current status of my open tickets?
You can view the status of all your tickets under the Tickets tab on the sidebar. The list provides status badges such as "Unassigned", "Assigned", and "Resolved". You can click on any specific ticket row to open its detailed view.
How do I add or update an item in the Inventory section?
Navigate to the Inventory tab. To add an item, click "Add New Item" at the top right. To update an existing item, click the three vertical dots (options menu) on the right side of the item's row and select "Edit Item". Ensure you save your changes before exiting.
What should I do if an inventory item is showing as "Out of Stock"?
If an item is out of stock, it should be immediately reported to the Procurement Department. You can also automatically generate a restock request directly from the Inventory detail page by selecting the "Request Restock" action button.
Where do I go to change my account password or staff profile details?
You can update your personal information by navigating to the Settings tab. From there, you can edit your profile details, manage security protocols (like changing your password), and adjust your notification preferences.
Who should I contact if I encounter a "System Error" message?
If you encounter a system error that prevents you from completing your work, please take a screenshot of the error and immediately utilize the "Report a Bug" button located in the Settings tab. A system administrator will review the error logs.
Is there a way to filter tickets by priority or date?
Yes! In the Tickets tab, you will find a filter icon (funnel) on the right side of the toolbar. Clicking this opens a panel where you can strictly filter tickets by Priority, Category, Status, Department, and assigned Agent.
